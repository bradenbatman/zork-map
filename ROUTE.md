# Zork I route to 350, played clean on 2026-09-29

The third full game reached 350 of 350 in 624 moves with no deaths. The two games before it could not, because every death costs ten points for good. This file is the route as it was actually played, in my own words, with the mistakes that cost time marked.

## Rules that decided the run

- Never die. A death takes ten points permanently, so one death already makes 350 impossible. The first game was spoiled by the thief, and a second attempt by walking into a dark room.
- Keep a lit light in hand before every step underground. The lantern and the ivory torch both work. Once the torch is banked, take it back out of the case before going down. Walking out of a dark room into another dark room is deadly, and a match lasts only about two moves, so matches are an emergency tool.
- The trap door is one way. From below, go home through the maze and the Cyclops passage, or up the Studio chimney.
- The chimney takes the brass lantern plus at most one other item. Going up with nothing is refused too.
- A carry limit applies by weight and by count (about seven items). The coffin and the trunk want almost empty hands.
- Fight strength is 2 plus one per 70 points of score. The thief is strength 5 and attacks on sight, so he is dealt with once the score is about 230 and the knife is in hand.
- Taking a treasure out of the case costs its case points until it goes back in.
- One save per account. Do not send `restart` unless a new run is wanted.

## Order that worked

1. **House.** North of House, Forest Path, up the tree for the egg, then the Kitchen through the window for the sack and lamp, the sword from the Living Room, rope and knife from the Attic. Move the rug and open the trap door. Light the lamp before going down.
2. **Troll.** Kill him with the sword (four rounds, no damage taken). Score 40 after the egg and troll.
3. **Painting and coins.** The Gallery painting is heavy, so drop the sword first. The skeleton room in the maze holds the key and a bag of coins. The thief walked through the Troll Room and lifted the painting and egg. That was fine, because he gave them back when he died.
4. **Cyclops.** Say the magic word at once. Home is then two steps east through the hole. Bank the coins.
5. **Bar, dam, reservoir.** Loud Room echo then the bar. Dam Lobby matchbook, Maintenance Room wrench and screwdriver, press only the yellow button. Turn the bolt at the Dam, wait about nine turns, and the trunk, pump and trident are free. Drop light items to carry the trunk. Home through the maze; score 114.
6. **Temple.** Round Room, Engravings Cave, Dome Room, tie the rope, down for the torch, down for the bell, south for candles and book, down twice to Hades. Ring the bell, relight the candles with a match, read the book, take the skull. Back north to the Round Room. Then the coffin from the Egyptian Room, back up to the Altar, and pray to arrive in the forest. Bank coffin, sceptre (out of the coffin), skull, torch; score 189.
7. **Pot.** Only with light hands. Take the sceptre out of the case, go to the End of Rainbow, wave it and take the pot in the same turn. Leaving the pot there lost it once. Waving twice undoes the rainbow. Score 209.
8. **River.** Inflate the boat at the Dam Base, board, launch, wait until the buoy, take it and land on the east beach. Open the buoy for the emerald, take the shovel, dig four times in the Sandy Cave for the scarab and stop there (a fifth dig is dangerous). Walk south to the falls and west over the rainbow. The pump and boat stay at the base. Score 234.
9. **Thief.** Bank first, then take the knife, a lit lantern, and a gift. He wounded me badly twice at strength 5 before the fight went my way in his lair, so retreat to the Living Room and rest at the first serious wound (about forty waits heals everything). The kill took a stun, a knockout and a last blow. His loot returned with the egg already opened. Bank the chalice, painting, coins, egg and canary. Score 295.
10. **Bauble.** Wind the canary on the Forest Path, take the bauble, bank both. Score 297.
11. **Mine.** From the Slide Room north. Squeaky Room, then the Bat Room with garlic in hand for the jade. Shaft Room, Smelly Room, put the torch down (no flame near gas), lantern on, down to the Gas Room for the bracelet. East, then northeast, southeast, southwest and down twice to the Dead End for the coal. Back up the same way. In the Shaft Room load the coal, screwdriver and torch into the basket and lower it. Walk round to the Timber Room, drop everything there with the lantern left lit, cross the crack, empty the basket, and in the Machine Room open the lid, put the coal in, close it, turn the switch with the screwdriver, open it and take the diamond. Put the diamond and torch back in the basket, raise it, collect the jade, bracelet and lantern from the Timber Room, climb round, and bring it all home by the Slide Room, the slide and the maze.
12. **Last bank.** Diamond, bracelet, jade and torch. The score reads 350 and a parchment map appears in the case. I stopped there and did not enter the barrow.
